**Open French word dictionaries and word lists for word games and language tools.** Free, documented, CSV first, on GitHub, Kaggle and Hugging Face.

### 👉 [Browse The Full Catalog, French Word Dictionaries And Word Lists](https://github.com/verbarium/french-word-dictionaries)

- 📖 [French dictionary](https://github.com/verbarium/french-dictionary) with definitions, gender, IPA and frequency
- 🎲 [French Wordle, Sutom and hangman word lists](https://github.com/verbarium/french-word-games) and [French word frequency](https://github.com/verbarium/french-word-frequency), by [Mot Valide](https://www.motvalide.fr/)
- 🧩 [French crossword clues](https://github.com/verbarium/french-crossword-clues) and [crosswordese](https://github.com/verbarium/french-crosswordese), by [La Petite Case](https://www.lapetitecase.fr/)
- 🔤 [Synonyms](https://github.com/verbarium/overused-words-6-languages) and [antonyms](https://github.com/verbarium/antonyms-6-languages) in six languages, by [Synopedia](https://www.synopedia.com/)
- 🔀 [Anagram statistics](https://github.com/verbarium/anagram-statistics), [families](https://github.com/verbarium/common-anagram-families) and an [LLM benchmark](https://github.com/verbarium/anagram-benchmark-llm), by [Anagrammio](https://www.anagrammio.com/)

Also on [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/cuivrax) and [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/verbarium).
