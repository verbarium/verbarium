**Open word data for word games and language tools.** Clean, documented word lists you can drop into a project, built from the data behind a family of free word sites. Every dataset is free, CSV first, and available on Kaggle and Hugging Face.

## 🎲 French Scrabble And Word Games, by [Mot Valide](https://www.motvalide.fr/)

| Dataset | Kaggle | Hugging Face | Notebook |
|---|---|---|---|
| French Wordle, Sutom and hangman word lists, best opening words | [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/cuivrax/french-word-games-starter-pack) | [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/verbarium/french-wordle-sutom-word-lists) | [Quick tour](https://www.kaggle.com/code/cuivrax/french-word-games-starter-pack-quick-tour) |
| French word frequency and Scrabble validity, 143,351 words | [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/cuivrax/french-word-frequency-scrabble-validity) | [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/verbarium/french-word-frequency-scrabble-validity) | [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/code/cuivrax/how-much-french-can-you-play-in-scrabble), [Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1KWYTdrSF1uhFIpYOKle0vLvfgfSgS0yV) |
